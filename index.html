Privacy Policy for Pour Decisions

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Pour Decisions ("the app") is developed by Rajesh Guntupalli.

Summary

Pour Decisions does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no backend server, and makes no network requests — everything happens on your device.

Data collection

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any other data, from any source

Data storage

Your game progress — current level, difficulty setting, stars, Drops, unlocked bottle skins, Daily Cocktail streak and Recipe Book progress, and your settings — is stored only in your device's local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never sent to us or to any third party
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the app

Third-party services

Pour Decisions does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder in Settings, the app schedules a local notification using your phone's own notification system. No server, push token, or data leaving your device is involved. You can turn it off at any time in Settings or in your phone's system settings.

Permissions

The app does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or files.

Children's privacy

Pour Decisions does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that collects data), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
